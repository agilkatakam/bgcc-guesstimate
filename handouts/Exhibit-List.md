# The twelve exhibits

Every different exhibit your team opens counts. Of the correct answers, the team that opened the fewest exhibits wins; if two teams opened the same number, the earlier submission wins.

1. **Post-mortem report.** The post-mortem on the body from the study, with the investigating officer's covering note naming his main suspect and listing who came through the estate gate that day.
2. **Seized in Panjim.** Goa Crime Branch file on a forger raided in Panjim: a passport issued on forged papers for one client, and the one-way flight from Mumbai booked with that passport.
3. **Insurance file.** Konkan Life's claim file on Aryan: his medical exam, the phone numbers he gave, his debts and his position in the family trust, from the insurer's side.
4. **Case diary, 8 November.** The investigating officer's diary for 8 November: the scene, what the post-mortem found, who identified the body, the gate register, and his lines of inquiry.
5. **A doctor's patient file.** Dr. Rao's clinic file on Aryan: his medical history, the one number he wanted to be called on, his debts and his position in the family trust, from the doctor's side.
6. **Call record for a company SIM.** A phone company's printout for one company mobile on the night of the fire: its calls and texts, the towers that carried them, whose account it is on, and a police note tracing the anonymous morning call.
7. **A lender's file.** Big Mishra Fund's credit file on Aryan: his medical check, the private number he gave, his debts and his position in the family trust, from the lender's side.
8. **Order releasing the remains.** The magistrate's order letting the family cremate the body, and everything it weighed first: the post-mortem, the identification, the gate log, and the police case.
9. **Flagged at the airport.** Mumbai airport police lookout for 9 November: one-way passengers flagged for a Goa case, one of them on a passport issued on forged papers, and where his ticket was bought.
10. **The CFO's phone, examined.** The cyber cell's report on the CFO's seized phone: one company mobile that contacted her that night and the towers it used, and a trace of the anonymous morning call.
11. **A travel agent's day book.** Papers police seized from a Mapusa travel agency: a cash-paid one-way flight ticket from Mumbai, a copy of the passport used to book it, and what the passport office said about it.
12. **Police control room log.** The police control room's log for the night of the fire and the morning after, with its trace replies: a company mobile's towers that night and the anonymous morning call.
