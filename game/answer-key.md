# Answer key (organisers only; loaded into the judges' screen)

**Check the opened exhibits first.** The exhibits fall into four groups: A = 1, 4, 8; B = 3, 5, 7; C = 6, 10, 12; D = 2, 9, 11. A claim can be ticked only if the team opened at least one exhibit from each group listed for it below, whatever the answer says. This stops a team that guessed, or overheard another team, from skipping a group. So no answer can be correct with fewer than 4 exhibits.

A submission is correct only if it makes ALL six claims below, with no contradicting claim (see the end). Any wording is fine, but each claim is ticked only if it states the specific facts listed; a claim that could be guessed from the case file alone does not count. Claims 1, 2, 3 and 5 normally appear under question 1, claim 4 under question 2, claim 6 under question 3, but a claim counts wherever it appears.

1. The body is not Aryan's: it has an old femur (thigh-bone) fracture fixed with a steel plate and screws, while Aryan's own medical history (insurance medical, Dr. Rao's file or the lender's key-man medical) shows he never had a fracture or surgery.
2. Dr. Rao faked the identification and brought the body: he supplied the dental chart it was matched to (or vouched that the plate was an old injury of Aryan's he had treated, which Aryan's own medicals show is a lie) AND his clinic van went to the outhouse on the afternoon of 7 Nov (the gate register says 3:10 to 3:45 PM; the exact time is not required). "Rao identified the body" or "Aryan asked for the van" alone is not enough.
3. Aryan was out of the study and alive: his phone 4471 rejected a call at 11:20 PM on the Siolim cell site, 3 km away, before the 11:40 fire, OR sent the 1:50 AM text from the Mapusa KTC bus stand cell site. "He left by the window" or "the text was sent at 1:50 AM" alone is not enough.
4. His reason to vanish, with at least one specific: Rs 360 cr owed to Sethi; repayment due 30 Nov; the trust's 62% pledged against it; or the DNA result (not Devraj's son) together with the removal vote on 21 Nov. "He owed Sethi money" or "Kabir wanted him out" alone is not enough.
5. He framed Tara, shown by EITHER of these: (a) the 1:50 AM "Done. As agreed." text to her came from Aryan's own phone 4471, sent to make her look like an accomplice; OR (b) the 6:10 AM tip pointing police at her was traced to Kankavli (or "Hotel Sahyadri", or "the NH66 dhaba where Goa–Mumbai buses halt") on his route north, and is presented as his doing or part of his diversion. "Likely" is fine.
6. He is escaping as "Arvind Menon" on the flight from Mumbai to Colombo, 9 November, 11:40 PM.

**Contradictions that fail an answer:** Tara knew he was alive, or helped him stage it or escape; Sethi, Pinto, Naina, Rohan or Kabir killed him; Rao killed Aryan; Aryan is dead; Menon is anyone but Aryan. These do NOT fail an answer: saying Tara's role is unclear; saying she was in the Halcyon fraud or blackmailed him (true); saying Naina may have known or helped with the fake death (nothing disproves it); wrong guesses about where the body came from or who the dead man was. Other wrong side details are ignored.

**If no answer is fully correct:** the answer with the most claims ticked wins. Ties go to fewer exhibits opened, then to the earlier submission.
