# CASE FILE

North Goa Police: preliminary brief, 8 November 2026

**Deceased:** Aryan Vardhan, 45, Managing Director and CEO of Vardhan Industries, a shipping and logistics group founded by his father, Devraj Vardhan.

**Incident:** On the night of Saturday 7 November, the Vardhan family hosted the company's Founders' Day party at their estate in Assagao, North Goa, with about 120 guests.

- 10:30 PM: A caterer sees Aryan walk alone to the outhouse study at the back of the grounds and go inside.
- 11:15 PM: A gardener sees Aryan's son, Rohan, near the outhouse.
- 11:40 PM: Guests notice the outhouse on fire.
- 11:55 PM: The fire brigade arrives. The door is found bolted from the inside. The rear window's wooden shutters are closed but unlatched.
- 12:30 AM: A body is recovered from the study.
- 8 November, 9:00 AM: After the post-mortem, the family physician, Dr. Anand Rao, formally identifies the remains as Aryan Vardhan.

**Status:** Treated as murder. Nobody at the party has a complete alibi between 10:30 and 11:40 PM. At the family's request, the remains are to be cremated on 9 November.

## Glossary
- **Call detail record (CDR):** the phone company's log of a number's calls and texts, with the cell tower ("cell site") that carried each one. A phone uses a nearby tower, so the cell site shows roughly where the phone was.
- **Larynx, trachea, bronchi:** the voice box and the airways below it, down to the lungs. A person breathing during a fire inhales soot into them.
- **Femur:** the thigh bone.
- **Pledge:** to offer shares as security for a loan; if the loan is not repaid, the lender takes them.
- **Managing Trustee:** the person who runs a family trust and votes the shares it holds.
- **Nominee:** the person an insurance policy pays out to.
- **Dhaba:** a roadside restaurant.
- **CFO:** Chief Financial Officer, the company's head of finance.
- **SIM:** the card in a mobile phone that gives it its number.
- **One-way ticket:** a ticket out with no return journey booked.
- **The board:** the company's board of directors, the group that oversees the company and can remove its top people.
- **SEBI:** India's stock-market regulator.
- **Halcyon:** Halcyon Trading, a company in the Vardhan group.
- **Post-mortem:** the medical examination of a dead body to find out how the person died.
- **Panchnama:** a police record of a scene or search, signed by witnesses.
- **Viscera:** internal organs, kept for lab tests.
- **IO:** Investigating Officer, the police officer in charge of the case.
- **PS, SP:** police station; Superintendent of Police (a senior police officer).
- **RPO:** Regional Passport Office, which issues passports.
- **PAN:** India's tax ID number, printed on a PAN card.
